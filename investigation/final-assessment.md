# Final Assessment

## Classification

**Phishing Attempt — PayPal Impersonation**

## Assessment

The combined evidence supports classifying the investigated
email as a phishing attempt impersonating PayPal.

The assessment is based on multiple independent indicators:

1. Look-alike sender domain using `paypa1`.
2. Different Reply-To domain.
3. Different Return-Path domain.
4. No SPF record identified by PhishTool.
5. No DKIM signature identified by PhishTool.
6. No DMARC record identified by PhishTool.
7. Multiple suspicious URLs.
8. VirusTotal phishing detections for two shortened URLs.
9. Urgent account-suspension messaging.
10. Requests for credential, financial, and identity
    information.
11. No attachment was identified.

The conclusion is based on the combined evidence rather than
a single indicator.
