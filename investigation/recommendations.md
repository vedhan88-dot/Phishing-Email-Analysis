# Recommendations

## 1. Block or Quarantine Indicators

Where appropriate, security controls should block or
quarantine confirmed phishing URLs and domains identified
during the investigation.

## 2. User Awareness

Users should be reminded not to provide passwords,
payment-card information, billing information, or identity
information in response to unsolicited account-verification
emails.

## 3. Verify Through Known Channels

Account-security or payment-related requests should be
verified through known official websites or applications
rather than links contained in suspicious emails.

## 4. Monitor Related Indicators

Security teams can monitor for related:

- Sender addresses
- Domains
- URLs
- IP addresses
- Similar email subjects

## 5. Document IOCs

The identified indicators should be retained in the
investigation record for correlation with future alerts.

## 6. Credential or Financial Information Exposure

If a recipient entered credentials or financial information
into a phishing page, the appropriate account-security and
incident-response procedures should be initiated.
